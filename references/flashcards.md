# Writing flashcards

Cards are for remembering what the video taught, a week later, without rewatching.

- **One fact or idea per card.** If the answer needs "and also…", split it.
- **Ask for the thing worth remembering**: frameworks (the three layers), mechanisms (how the sentinel agent works), numbers with their source, the speaker's position on a question, a vivid example and what it illustrates.
- **Mix types**: what/why/how questions, "fill in" (`哪一类行业位于监管光谱最左端？`), contrast (`可扩展监督和递归自我改进用于对齐，区别是什么？`), and 2–3 "your take" cards that ask the reader to apply an idea (`如果你在设计一个 AI 助理，你会怎么用“哨兵代理”的思路？` with a model answer).
- **Answers are short**: one to three sentences. Add the timestamp (`time`) so the reader can jump back to the video.
- **Tag** cards with the section/theme so they can be grouped (`tag`).
- Avoid trivia nobody needs (what shirt someone wore) and questions whose answer is in the question.

12–25 cards for a one-hour video.
